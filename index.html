Robert Arons

Economist · Industrial organization and antitrust

I am an economist working on industrial organization and antitrust. My research is empirical and applied, covering bargaining and firm size in the pay-TV industry, competition and cost sharing in health care, and the effects of education policy.

I earned my PhD in Economics from the University of Rochester in 2014.

Research interests

  • Industrial organization
  • Labor economics
  • Health economics
  • Public economics
